# What the computation boundary establishes

ComputationBoundary proves a failure of the explicitly defined
ResourceOnlyAgreement contract, not an impossibility of the proposed internal
consistency theorem. The rejected source is the serialized, fully decoded
empty-context application certificate CheckerComparisons.bad_application.
The alternative residual expression is Compare q q, retaining the complete
source in both operands. Thus its true output comes from the actual structural
comparator, not an inserted whole-checker answer.

Resources includes actual original-PTS generation witnesses for the source,
budget and expression data, actual capacity witnesses for source/expression,
typed expression and execution terms, and generated Zip/capacity witnesses
for every recursive comparison operand and payload. resources_realized supplies
these independent facts for any source and residual expression in a common
finite storage envelope. The expression computes true, while the unchanged
source checker computes false. Their PTS execution terms are not even beta
convertible. Requiring successful decoding therefore does not repair the
weaker contract.

Concretely, the application certificate supplies a sort certificate as its
function premise. The original application rule must compare that premise's
sort type with the required product type; they differ. Self-comparison of the
source never performs that required rule check, although all its stored data
and every actual comparison traversal are structurally valid.

The rejection of this concrete source is a direct computation of the unchanged
checker. Boolean separation uses the existing normal-point/confluence argument;
no general native consistency or Bottom-emptiness theorem is used to establish
the counterexample.

The missing binding is operational provenance: the local source rules must
determine exactly which comparisons, endpoint computations, context lookups,
substitutions and conjunctions occur. An independently defined local compiler
graph can provide that binding, but its coverage and internal agreement must
be proved. Inserting the intended agreement equation into resource admission
would assume the disputed bridge.

## Separate dependent-soundness gate

Even correct residual execution does not imply that a Bottom checker rejects.
FiniteFragmentNormalization proves external bounded normalization by composing
certificate_sound with fixed_rank_peak_normalization; its final rejection uses
NativeCanonicity.normal_void_empty. These native proofs do not themselves
construct the internal implication from represented acceptance to a preserved
normalization invariant.

InternalReplacement and ReplacementRealization supply scope preservation and
canonical capture-avoiding substitution witnesses. They do not yet prove
dependent typing preservation under represented, typed environments. For a
closure/stack presentation, the first substantive beta law is agreement of the
body's annotated environment/type closure with the application's substituted
codomain closure, including supplied conversion instructions.

The known rank obstruction applies to a proposed same-index proof pattern:
an implication whose domain has rank r cannot also have rank r. Increasing
every index together preserves that mismatch. A whole-history fold or a
history-stable closure invariant could avoid this particular pattern; neither
has been ruled out by the counterexample. Its local dependent beta-splicing
and conversion laws still require an actual internal derivation.

One cannot strengthen admission by requiring all conversion intermediates to
normalize. InvariantObstructions gives an accepted native certificate with an
untyped, non-strongly-normalizing intermediate in its supplied equality path.

The existing finite-Horn alternative also does not silently complete the
bridge. FiniteModelSoundness translates proofs relative to an explicit model
premise. HilbertFiniteModels obtains its canonical Void denial using external
native soundness and Void emptiness; the Hilbert catalogue's completeness for
all original native derivations remains open. The finite-ungraded-native-model
obstruction does not exclude growing guarded fragments or weaker finite models.
