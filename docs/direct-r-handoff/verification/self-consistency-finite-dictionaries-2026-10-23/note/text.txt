A direct route to internal consistency of PTS-rk
October 23, 2026
Abstract
We propose a direct, resource-relativized consistency proof in the original PTS-rk,
without interpreting H<ω or constructing an Elementary Arithmetic derivation. The
starting points are the independent native certificate checker, external graded normaliza-
tion and canonicity, and the new internal PHOAS fold interface. The principal missing
construction is one fixed-rank internal soundness term for arbitrary represented certificates
with independently certified resources. A continuation-encoded resource domain then gives
a short negative consistency proof. This is a roadmap, not a claim that internal soundness
or consistency has already been proved. The checked Rocq infrastructure establishes
external adequacy of independent native certificates, faithful homogeneous serialization,
and compression of complete contextual inputs with labels bounded by serialized input
size. It now also supplies an actual fixed-rank code carrier, generation witnesses, and an
executable schema of internal rejection proofs for every externally size-bounded complete
certificate fragment. Each rejection type has rank four. A rank-five polymorphic consumer
now eliminates represented Church dictionaries through one fixed interface. Native-checker
branch generation and resource coverage are still external, not an internal all-budget theo-
rem. The supporting development includes fixed-rank resource lookup/prefix/extension
laws, fixed-rank histories, structural capacity, and consumer-directed preservation with
standard-code realizations.
1 Recommendation and current starting point
Prioritize proof-local normalization and relational contradiction separation, not full
finite models of every rank fragment. This uses the finite resources needed by a supplied
proof and avoids stronger semantic closure obligations that are unnecessary for a negative
consistency result.
There is an important distinction in the current combinatory note1. Its normalization results
give graded finite syntactic carriers: application can enlarge the grade, and valuations are
typed syntactic substitutions. The updated Rocq development also proves native normal-form
canonicity, Void-denying finite ground-Horn models and finite semantic proof realization.
These are distinct interfaces. In particular, FiniteNativeModelBoundary rules out a single
ungraded finite term carrier with total compositional application, full native typing/beta
soundness, semantic conversion and empty Void. This does not rule out growing, genuinely
bounded fragments. We therefore use the unchanged native checker on complete size-bounded
certificates, not a finite quotient model of the entire calculus.
The useful existing interfaces are:
• NativeCertificates: independent six-rule certificates, explicit annotated beta instruc-
tions, accepted-typing, certify, and certificate-adequacy.
• NativeRankCoverage, NativeNormalization: computed sufficient bounds, total typed
normalization, conversion adequacy and budget independence.
• FragmentModelSoundness, FragmentPackages: soundness for independently accepted con-
textual inputs and joint finite bounds for represented data.

1.1 Checked certificate and compression milestone
The library PTSrk.SelfConsistency uses the original kernel, Stdlib, Equations and the shared
annotated assembler/native checker. Neither arithmetic interpretation nor a set model is
imported. NativeProofCodes supplies executable acceptance for all six rules, annotated beta
locations, equality trees and context declaration certificates. The adequacy theorem in
CertificateAdequacy proves that an accepted certificate with specified endpoints exists exactly
when the original typing judgment holds. Context acceptance is separately sound and complete
for original well-formedness. Positive examples exercise the rules; negative examples reject
forged ranks, variable types, context declarations and mismatched equality endpoints.
SortRenaming proves commutation with weakening and substitution, preservation of arbi-
trary beta conversion, and preservation of certificate acceptance. The coverage theorem in
RankCompression then compresses every original derivable judgment in a well-formed context.
Its support includes the certificates for context declarations, all annotations, rule ranks, com-
puted product ranks and equality instructions. Compression fixes 0, 1 and ⊥, preserves term
node counts, and is injective on that support. StructuralCodes and CertificateSerialization
serialize the entire input into one tree type with a fixed finite alphabet and canonical binary
labels and indices. Executable decoders check scope and recover every annotated constructor.
Both inverse laws hold: encoding then decoding recovers the source, and every successfully
decoded tree re-encodes to that exact tree. Thus no field is silently ignored or repaired, and
encoding is injective. The raw checker is sound and covers every original derivable judgment
in a well-formed context. Raw compression preserves acceptance; its parsing requirement is
independent of acceptance, so well-scoped rejected inputs can also be compressed.
SerializationBounds proves that every compressed label is at most the complete serialized
input’s node count. Binary label payloads, annotations, context certificates, conversion
witnesses and repeated fields are charged. These are external theorems. Internal decoder
relations, operation-cost and proof/history-size bounds remain to construct. Malformed trees
are ordinary raw codes that the decoder rejects; their eventual resource admission must not
depend on successful parsing or acceptance.

2 Freeze a direct consistency statement
Write T= PTSrk and ⊥= ΠX : U0.X : U1. All final terms use the original annotated syntax,
typing and beta conversion: no primitives, cumulativity or eta rule.
Choose a homogeneous structural code type D for complete finite certificates. A code includes
its context data, annotations, rule ranks, conversion instructions and any checker-trace
witnesses. Thus quantifying over the whole code also quantifies over every trace; no trusted
trace is silently supplied by the metatheory. A finite constructor alphabet can represent
arbitrary binary labels as trees without turning those labels into actual universe literals during
interpretation.
Define an independent predicate Acc⊥(q) meaning that the raw certificate checks and its
conclusion is a closed term of the displayed syntactic type ⊥. It must contain local syntax/graph
checks, not native Typing.typ, successful normalization, or a semantic soundness assumption.
The proposed source is the existing native certificate grammar. It is not the draft’s distinct
T e calculus with typed judgmental equality. Develop its general judgment checker first and
specialize its root to ⊥afterward. Positive acceptance tests and adequacy must cover ordinary
inhabited judgments, not only the externally empty contradiction case.
Choose another fixed structural carrier B for resource descriptions and an independently
defined predicate Res(q, b). It certifies well-generated data, size/rank envelopes and sufficient
finite capacity. It must not assert rejection of q, existence of a normalizing history, or validity
of a model of the source theory. These are conclusions to prove. Here well-generated means
structural code validity, not acceptance or well-formedness of the encoded typing context;
rejected standard inputs must also admit resources.
All carriers, graph predicates and proof types must be well formed at one fixed finite
collection of PTS ranks, independent of the represented source ranks, certificate lengths
and budgets. This is a construction obligation, not a consequence of representing a number by
a term.
2.1 The central obligation and a short final assembly
The central target, (BSdir), is an actual closed original PTS term
bs : Πq : D.Πb : B.Res(q, b) →Acc⊥(q) →⊥.
Define the admitted domain by a fixed-answer-universe resource package:
Adm(q) := ΠZ : U1.(Πb : B.Res(q, b) →Z) →Z,
Consdir := Πq : D.Adm(q) →Acc⊥(q) →⊥.
Since ⊥: U1, the proposed final proof is simply
λq a h. a ⊥(λb r.bs q b r h).
This is a proof plan, not an assumption of bs. Every displayed type still needs its original
formation derivation. The package permits elimination into ⊥without extracting a positive
resource witness from double negation. Neither a uniform function choosing a budget nor a
normal-form output inside the admitted domain is needed.

To make this a meaningful consistency interpretation, prove separately:
1. Every standard finite raw certificate, accepted or not, has an encoding in the same D
and an Adm proof. Construct explicit finite resources; do not use external consistency to
establish admission.
2. Construct an actual internal acceptance proof for each independently accepted standard
certificate, including conversion and trace data; a Boolean computation with value
true is insufficient. Internal laws must also hold for arbitrary guarded inputs, not just
quotations.
3. The source certificate system covers every original typing derivation. Any conversion to
another certificate format preserves the relevant predicates and resource guards in the
direction being used.
For this negative direction, forward acceptance realization suffices: an independent overapprox-
imation is safe if bounded soundness handles every represented acceptance proof. Reverse
agreement is additionally needed to identify the exact native checker, the stronger specification
retained here. Realization must cover general judgments constructively, not use external
emptiness of the Bottom case. Pointwise standard coverage is not an internal implication
between consistency sentences; that requires a uniform internal acceptance/domain translation.
This specifies a resource-relativized structural consistency sentence. It is not automatically
the old J (NAT(C)), nor unrestricted arithmetical consistency. If separate proof and trace
variables are retained, prove the joint-resource lemma from their individual guards. Packaging
the entire input is a deliberate choice of sentence, not a free equivalence.

4 Roadmap and acceptance gates
4.1 Independent certificates and the exact target
The external six-rule certificate format, raw equality trees with explicit redex locations, scope
decoding, checker soundness/completeness and faithful structural serialization are checked.
All lambda annotations are retained. Conversion verification uses supplied instructions, with
no normalization oracle. The size L counts the complete serialized tree; repeated fields are
charged on expansion. Next realize these relations inside fixed-rank PTS carriers and specify
D, B, resource validity and the final sentence before building internal soundness.
Gate: all six rule cases have independent acceptance clauses; malformed contexts, wrong
substitutions, mismatched conversion endpoints and forged rank data are rejected. No
acceptance premise is the desired interpretation. If the draft’s exact T e sentence is required
later, add checked certificate translations and guard comparison rather than appealing to
informal equivalence of typing systems.
4.2 Elementary compression of source ranks
Collect every sort label in the complete certificate, including contexts, annotations and equality
paths, together with all rule ranks and used computed product-result ranks. Adjoin 0, 1. Let
σ enumerate the distinct labels in increasing order. For every used product equation,
c = max(a + 1, b) = ⇒ σ(c) = max(σ(a) + 1, σ(b)).
If c = a + 1, no integer can lie between the two labels, so their new indices are consecutive.
If c = b > a, strict order suffices. This is not a claim that arbitrary increasing renamings
preserve successor everywhere. Prove that renaming commutes with weakening, substitution
and annotated beta steps; lift it through the entire accepted certificate. It fixes ⊥.
The number of new labels is linear in L. Compressed terms have unchanged tree shapes, and
the existing sufficient redex-bound expression 1 + maxRankLiteral(t) + nodes(t) becomes O(L)
for the displayed source terms. Thus the existing elementary bounds suggest a tower envelope of
polynomial height in complete input size, without paying for the numeric magnitude of binary
labels. Bounds for constructed proofs, substitutions and histories must still be established
separately.
Gate: prove both the external original-typing theorem and an internal relational compression
theorem on guarded codes. Source-rank compression does not compress an externally indexed

4.5 One structural resource interface
Represent budgets, finite sequences, table entries and schedules as data in fixed carriers. A
varying finite dictionary or table must be a value of a fixed type, not an externally generated
telescope with an unbounded number of fields. The resource relations, history iteration and
structural capacity below are checked; the complete resource envelope remains to internalize.
Checked growth and joining. InternalResources retains the same C : U2. For P : C →U4,

L(P ) = Πa : C.Πb : C. Good1(b) →P (a) →P (pair(a, b)),
R(P ) = Πa : C.Πb : C. Good1(a) →P (b) →P (pair(a, b)),

to the closure rules. Pairing joins two generated resources: it is generated and extends each

4.6 Which obligations are actually necessary?
Global lookup functionality and matching-constructor inversion are useful for extracting and
comparing decoded fields, but are not intrinsic to relational soundness. No implemented
consumer currently needs their full statements. Instead, define operation and checker relations
by independent constructor-indexed clauses, retaining their fields, and fold a supplied witness
directly into the invariant required by its consumer. This must preserve the invariant for every
related output, not merely produce one compatible output. Bind a result once and thread
that same variable through dependent consumers; combining separately obtained results still
requires a consumer-specific coherence theorem. Independent local acceptance clauses and
forward realization of all six original typing rules are not weakened.
Checked consumer-directed transport. RelationalTransport supplies original PTS terms
for P : C →U4. If P descends from a pair to either child when the sibling is generated, then
Look5(a, p, v) →P (a) →P (v).

growth, lookup transports a refutation of P (a) to a refutation of P (v). Both laws extend to

arguments, not an already constructed typing or normalization invariant.

Three further requirements can be weakened at the design level:
• Same-rank lookup composition: already bypassed by independently witnessed seg-
ments and fixed-rank histories. No arbitrary rank lifting is needed for that construction.
• Internal numerical capacity comparison: optional if structural operation-closure
certificates establish the actual sufficiency needed by soundness. External bounds may
construct resources for every standard input; finite-family packing alone does not establish
that sufficiency. The final continuation package does not need an internal budget selector.
• Rebuilding complete typing certificates after every reduction: a sufficient
independently defined semantic or typing invariant may be preserved instead. Every
represented object actually constructed must still be charged, and dependent substitu-
tion/conversion remain to prove.
The concrete laws above establish these bypasses only for their stated consumers. They are not
yet a replacement proof of bounded soundness. The next decisive task is the actual proof-node
invariant, with exact motive ranks and local binder, substitution and conversion preservation.
An envelope must account for the input, compressed rank bound, annotations, substitution
images, reduction states, conversion data, proof transport and any history actually represented.
Closure facts concern these independently specified operations. Neither arbitrary semantic
table correctness nor existence of a successful normalization history belongs in the envelope.
Prove admission of every standard raw input by constructing a sufficiently large finite resource
diagram. Internally derive exactly the prefix, subcertificate and joining laws used in soundness.
Do not silently demand closure of the admitted domain under every successor, proof concate-
nation or tower construction. If the representation needs a fixed amount of spare capacity,
supply it in the resource contract; paired relational resources are an optional repair requiring
their own correspondence proof.
Remaining gate: specify the concrete proof-node invariant and prove preservation for every
output of the required operation relations. Establish operation-specific structural sufficiency
and standard raw-input admission. Prove functionality, inversion or numerical comparison only
if an identified consumer needs it; the current structural graphs and finite-table generation
alone do not pass the complete resource gate.

4.8 Small terminal separation and bounded soundness
Internalize the normal-form obstruction for ⊥. A normal closed function of type ΠX : U0.X

4.9 Adequacy, relational comparison and the incompleteness audit
Audit standard raw-code admission and checker agreement independently of soundness. For
a conventional predicate-only presentation, interpret atomic relations by the proved graph
predicates and quantify every trace witness. If returning to the historical sentence, prove
certificate translation, domain correspondence, resource joining and the implication needed to
transport consistency internally. Agreement on standard numerals alone does not identify two
internal statements.
Run the logical viability audit early, alongside steps 1–4. Avoiding arithmetic notation does
not avoid Gödel’s second incompleteness theorem. If this coding supports the applicable
diagonal fixed point and the Hilbert–Bernays–Löb conditions, a consistent theory cannot prove
the corresponding consistency statement. Determine which domain/closure or derivability
conditions are actually available; do not simply declare one to fail. A relational presentation
and separate admission of every standard code do not automatically supply uniform successor or
proof-combination closure. Conversely, their absence cannot merely be assumed. Pakhomov’s
introduction discusses this distinction3

4.10 Weak finite satisfiability and the non-Gödelianity question
The weak local finite satisfiability result in the combinatory note, Section sec-ground-
horn4 is probably highly relevant to investigating a non-Gödelian consistency interpretation
of PTS-rk. This is a research direction, not an established implication. Here “non-Gödelian”
means a precisely specified boundary-case self-justification property, not completeness or an
exception to Gödel’s theorem with all its hypotheses intact.
The original positive-Horn result alone admitted the all-true model and could not separate
contradiction. The updated HilbertFiniteModels closes that particular gap: its least models
validate the selected ground rules and reject every closed Void judgment, using proved native
normalization and canonicity. Ground predicates still form an infinite recursive vocabulary,
not a faithful finite-signature quotient of native syntax. Exhaustive finite constructor search
and a weaker quantified example are also proved, but the example omits two essential axioms
and is not a full PTS model. The separate native finite-model impossibility theorem explains
why a size-guarded or graded interface is essential to the present route.
Dan E. Willard’s stability framework5, especially Definition 5.10 and Theorem 5.11, gives
sufficient conditions for consistent self-justifying extensions. Its conditions control truth
of short deductions, rather than merely existence of finite models. The deduction method
matters: his tableaux analysis6, Theorem 4.1 and the discussion following it, does not extend
unchanged to Hilbert or Tab-2 deduction. Calling our combinator presentation “Hilbert-style”
does not establish a match with any of these proof predicates.
The proposed connection is to combine these genuine contradiction-denying finite interpretations
with explicit bounds relating proof size to the available resources. Adding¬p⊥ as an unexplained
model assumption would still only restate the missing problem. The required bridge has three
parts: an independent bounded truth interpretation; its quantitative preservation through
represented deductions; and realization of that argument as the internal all-budget soundness
term. These are precisely places where rank compression and resource-relative admission
may help. The new rejection schema supplies internal finite cases, but not the all-budget
preservation or the stability hypotheses by itself.
Finally, the target remains the unchanged PTS-rk calculus. A theorem about a theory
extended by a self-consistency axiom is not a derivation of Consdir here. We must construct its
inhabitant or prove an exact interpretation/transfer theorem, while auditing the domain closure
and derivability conditions. No self-consistency axiom is introduced. The working hypothesis
and precise literature checklist are recorded in docs/direct-r-handoff/DECISIONS.md.
