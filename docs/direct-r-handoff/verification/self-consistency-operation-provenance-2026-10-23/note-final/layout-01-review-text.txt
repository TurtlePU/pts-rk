A direct route to internal consistency of PTS-rk
October 23, 2026
Abstract
The goal is an actual original-PTS inhabitant of a resource-relativized consistency
statement, without using H<ω or Elementary Arithmetic as intermediate proof theories.
The development already supplies faithful certificate codes, source-rank compression, an
internal rejection proof for every externally bounded raw-code fragment, and fixed internal
dictionary and comparison operations. Independent local source graphs now prove source-
to-operation agreement externally, and one fixed internal term proves residual execution
agreement for arbitrary represented execution witnesses. Uniform internal source soundness
remains open: represented lookup, substitution and endpoint provenance must replace the
native operations still used in the source graph. This note separates these obligations from
resource coverage and the later finite-fragment preservation argument. It is not a claim of
internal consistency.
1 Target and proof obligations
Use the original annotated syntax, Typing.typ, and Ranks.RankSorts throughout. In particular,
Ui : U0, rank(Πx : A.B) = max(a + 1, b) (A : Ua, B : Ub), ⊥ = ΠX : U0.X : U1.
There is no cumulativity, eta rule, new primitive, or assumed soundness principle. A Rocq
theorem that no native term inhabits ⊥ is an external result; the target here is a closed PTS
term of a specified consistency type.
Let D be a fixed carrier of complete proof codes and B a fixed carrier of resources. Codes include
contexts, annotations, binary ranks, conversion instructions and any supplied computation
witnesses. Define independently:
• Acc⊥(q): the source certificate passes its local rule checks and concludes a closed term
at the literal syntactic type ⊥;
• Res(q, b): structural generation, envelopes and the operation capacities needed for the
proof.
Acceptance must not contain native typing or semantic soundness as a field. Resources must
not assert rejection, validity of a source model, or existence of a normalizing history. These
are conclusions to derive. Standard rejected and malformed codes must also receive resources.
The main remaining theorem is an actual term
bs : Πq : D.Πb : B. Res(q, b) → Acc⊥(q) → ⊥. (BS)
All its carriers and quantified types must use one fixed finite collection of ranks, independently
of represented source ranks, proof lengths and budgets.

1.1 Final resource elimination
The following continuation package avoids demanding a uniform budget selector:
Adm(q) := ΠZ : U1. (Πb : B.Res(q, b) → Z) → Z,
Consdir := Πq : D.Adm(q) → Acc⊥(q) → ⊥.
Once (BS) is constructed, the proposed final term is
λq a h. a ⊥ (λb r.bs q b r h).
Explicit resources introduce admission by λZ k.k b r. For D : Ud, B : Ue, Res(q, b) : Ur
and Acc⊥(q) : Us, the calculated formation ranks are max(e + 2, r + 2) for Adm(q) and
max(d + 1, e + 3, r + 3, s + 1) for Consdir. These final builders and their original formation
derivations remain obligations, not consequences of the displayed notation.
Adequacy has three separate requirements: admission of every standard raw code, actual
internal acceptance witnesses for every accepted standard judgment, and completeness of the
source certificates for original typing. The third is already proved externally. The first two
must not be obtained by eliminating the externally impossible Bottom case.
2 Completed foundations and their exact scope
The library PTSrk.SelfConsistency imports the original kernel and shared annotated assem-
bler/checker, not an arithmetic interpretation or set model. Its detailed module inventory is in
README.md; verification commands and assumptions are in EVIDENCE.md.
• Certificates and serialization: an independent six-rule checker, original-typing ade-
quacy and faithful annotated raw codes, proved externally.
• Rank compression: complete inputs are relabeled; checking and Bottom are preserved;
labels are bounded by serialized size. This is not internal all-source soundness.
• Syntactic operations: fixed-rank lookup, histories, scope, renaming and annotated
substitution relations with standard witnesses. Scope is not dependent typing preserva-
tion.
• Finite Bottom rejection: rank-four internal rejection at every external complete-code
size bound, as an externally generated schema.
• Dictionaries: one rank-five-type polymorphic consumer for represented lists and inde-
pendent membership evidence. Rejecting branch packages are still externally supplied.
• Residual execution: actual structural comparison and conjunction with exact native-
checker output on compiled standard inputs. Source compilation remains external.
2.1 Why the finite schema is already a genuine internal result
For an external bound b, enumerate all raw codes
Qb = {q | codeSize(q) ≤ b}.
Admission does not test parsing or acceptance. The size counts complete certificates, including
annotations, binary fields and conversion witnesses. Decoding this finite family supplies one
conservative rank, subject-size bound and finite label alphabet. The improved combinatory
normalization results then cover every state along the chosen normalization of every decoded

accepted closed subject in a common finite syntactic carrier, retaining the original concluding
type.
Those normalization and canonicity arguments are external Rocq proofs. Their rejection
equations certify a finite table of the unchanged Bottom checker raw_bottom_check. For
selectors ei : Eb naming all entries and this Bottom-checker table fb, an independent finite-case
guard is
Canb(x) = ΠP : Eb → U2. P (e0) → · · · → P (ek−1) → P (x) : U3.
With Holds(v) = v U1 ⊤ ⊥, each branch of P (x) = Holds(fbx) → ⊥ converts to ⊥ → ⊥. Finite
elimination gives an actual original-PTS term
cb : Πx : Eb.Canb(x) → Holds(fbx) → ⊥, type(cb) : U4.
This handles arbitrary guarded inputs, not just canonical selectors. Quotation remains beta-
injective at each bound. However, the carrier, table and branch proof are chosen externally
from b.
Represented Church dictionaries remove the varying case telescope from the consumer: a
separate All-package holds branch proofs, while Guard/Fill express independent membership
and unused-branch filling. One internal eliminator consumes them. It does not construct All
from an arbitrary represented budget. In particular, the identity cannot inhabit Holds(f x) → ⊥
for symbolic f, x merely by unfolding a fold. The implemented dictionary parameter is E : U1;
integration with the raw carrier C : U2 still needs an exact-rank adaptation.
3 Internal computation and represented resources
3.1 Comparisons retain their operands
CheckerComparisons compiles the unchanged checker into
e ::= Truth(b) | Compare(q, r) | Both(e, e
′).
It covers all six rules, contexts, conversion instructions and malformed inputs. Compari-
son leaves contain full raw operands, not precomputed equality answers. Parsing, context
lookup and computing endpoints, including substitution, currently occur during this external
compilation.
InternalCodeComparison provides an independent traversal Zip(x, y) : U4 over the raw carrier
C : U2. Its clauses describe an atom versus arbitrary input, a pair versus an atom, and two
pairs with corresponding child traversals. None contains a Boolean answer or rejection claim.
One comparator of full-type rank five folds these clauses into actual tag tests and conjunctions.
Its root test opens the Boolean answer type X : U0 before folding C; it does not treat Bool : U1
as an admissible U0 fold argument.
A rank-three conjunction-expression carrier and a fixed evaluator of full-type rank four execute
those comparisons. On the externally compiled standard input, CheckerExecution proves exact
forward reduction:
run(compile(q)) β point(b) ⇐⇒ rawCheck(q) = b.

The Bottom checker has the analogous theorem. Both Boolean outcomes are retained. Raw
serialization/quotation is faithful, but residual checking programs need not be: different sort
certificates can yield the same program.
3.2 Joint traversal: checked interface and remaining construction
The new representation records joint alignment as data. Write
J = ΠX : U2. (C → X)29 → (C → C → X)29 → (X → X → X) → X : U4.
The exponent abbreviates a finite telescope of case arguments. The first case family records an
atom on the left and the complete right operand; the second records a left pair and right atom;
the third combines child traversals. Folding at X= C gives actual projections L, R : J → C.
The literal carrier, constructors, projections and independent guard GoodJ(j) : U6 have checked
original formation/typing derivations. The intended next operation is
buildZip : Πj : J.GoodJ(j) → Zip(L(j), R(j)), type(buildZip) : U7.
This operation and its standard realization are not yet verified. Its proposed motive is
j → Zip(Lj, Rj) : U4; the existing Zip constructors suggest a single fold, avoiding a nested fold
requiring unavailable child generation guards. Large conversion checks exhausted resources
during the attempted proof; that is a proof-engineering limitation, not a mathematical
obstruction to the construction.
Only the verified interface remains in the library. Unverified constructor and realization drafts
are kept separately as design evidence. Even the proposed operation constructs traversal
evidence from represented joint data; aligning two independent raw inputs and proving
source-checker agreement would remain separate obligations.
3.3 Storage bounds now include full operands
CheckerResourceBounds records both complete operands at every synchronized comparison
node. For its raw traversal data T (q, r),
|T (q, r)| ≤ (3 + |q| + |r|) min(|q|, |r|).
The residual-data encoding retains every expression constructor and operand; it is injective
even though the executed program need not be. Exact size accounting and original-PTS
capacity witnesses cover the source, expression data, every comparison operand and every
recursive traversal payload. One externally constructed envelope covers a whole complete-code
size-bounded fragment, including malformed and rejected sources.
These are storage results, not bounds on emitted PTS terms, reduction costs, or the compiler’s
intermediate allocations. The envelope itself is still generated externally. It does not assert
that the stored expression is the checker program belonging to the stored source.

4 Source provenance and internal execution
4.1 Independent source graphs
Resource coverage does not determine a program’s meaning. In ComputationBoundary, a scoped
rejected source q has actual generation, capacity and traversal witnesses, while Compare(q, q)
returns true. This refutes the resource-only agreement contract in Rocq, not the whole
consistency programme or an internally quoted universal sentence.
CheckerSourceGraph repairs this contract at the native level. Its local inductive clauses
record source fields, beta/equality endpoints, context lookup, all six typing-rule checks,
context checks and both raw wrappers. Matching nodes retain the two actual operands and
conjunction topology. No clause assumes acceptance, native typing or evaluator/checker
agreement. Induction proves agreement with the unchanged compiler, uniqueness, coverage
of every raw source, and both Boolean outcomes. Consequently, adding this graph to the
independently generated storage package excludes the self-comparison counterexample.
Original substitution, context lookup, encoding and decoding in these clauses are still native
operations. This graph is a specification with proved standard coverage, not yet its internal
PTS representation.
4.2 One internal execution-agreement term
For the existing expression carrier E : U3 and Bool : U1, InternalExecutionGraph independently
represents the local clauses
Run(e, b) Run(f, c)
Run(leaf(b), b),
Run(both(e, f ), and(b, c)).
Here b, c are arbitrary Boolean programs, not necessarily canonical selectors. The clauses
contain no evaluator equation. A Church fold with exact motive rank three gives Run(e, b) : U5.
Define
Same(b, c) := ΠP : Bool → U1. P (b) → P (c) : U3.
The checked original-PTS term
agree : Πe : E.Πb : Bool. Run(e, b) → Same(evaluate(e), b)
has full-type rank six and applies to arbitrary represented execution witnesses. Its
proof folds the local clauses, using the evaluator’s beta laws and predicate transport through
conjunction. This is exact-rank Leibniz transport, not arbitrary-rank equality or beta equality.
Taking P= Holds yields an internal acceptance-transport term and one rank-six-type rejection
operation
Πe : E. Run(e, false) → Holds(evaluate(e)) → ⊥.
False execution must first be derived; it is not resource admission and does not itself establish
source rejection.

CheckerGraphRealization constructs execution witnesses by recursion on residual syntax. Their
outputs are the symbolic conjunctions of actual comparison programs, not precomputed checker
answers. Applying the single internal agreement term and the native source graph gives exact
standard realizations for both outputs. Ordinary accepted judgments receive actual inhabitants
of residual acceptance; false outputs receive conditional rejection inhabitants. No external
Bottom-emptiness argument is used. Raw quotation remains faithful; neither residual programs
nor these execution witnesses are claimed to encode sources injectively.
4.3 Represented operations and the remaining boundary
Original context lookup now has a separate represented operation graph. Literal contexts
are encoded by empty and ext(g, A); these are not certificate-context codes, which also store
declaration proofs and ranks. The native erasure between them is explicit. The lookup clauses
perform one annotated shift at each crossed extension, including index zero:
Shiftn,n+1(A, A′)
Lookup(n + 1, ext(g, A), 0, A′),
Lookup(n, g, i, A) Shiftn,n+1(A, A′)
Lookup(n + 1, ext(g, B), i + 1, A′).
The displayed successors abbreviate explicit represented successor edges. Lookup fibers have
rank 18; a rank-19-type term derives output scope from an arbitrary lookup witness. Whole-
context scoping is a separate rank-14 guard: the zero clause need not inspect its prefix.
Executable standard witnesses follow the original Context.index exactly and retain injective
context quotation. These facts do not prove arbitrary-output functionality, dependent typing,
or internal certificate-context erasure.
For comparison, a proof-indexed relation Trace(x, y, z, b) : U6 retains the actual witness
z : Zip(x, y). Its local clauses specify the corresponding Zip constructor and symbolic tag test
or conjunction, not evaluator agreement. Formation and all three local Boolean-agreement
cases are checked for arbitrary open inputs, with child agreement as induction data. The
full constructor/induction assembly and its intended agreement term remain unverified. A
separate executable symbolic-output compiler has the correct beta law for every raw operand
pair, including malformed trees. Standard beta agreement is not a substitute for the missing
arbitrary-trace theorem.
The represented source graph should retain context, certificate, subject, type and expression
indices, sharing each chosen endpoint across its uses. Most endpoint clauses only assemble
syntax. The substantive operations are:
• context lookup’s endpoint law and its connection to certificate-context erasure, beyond
the proved standard witnesses and output-scope law;
• annotated substitution for application and beta-contraction endpoints, with the coherence
needed by their comparison consumers;
• product-rank computation and complete certificate/context parsing, including malformed
failures.
Existing replacement evidence proves arbitrary-output scope preservation and realizes the exact
standard substitution. It does not prove arbitrary endpoint coherence; its general environment
can be multivalued. Restrict to the actual single-substitution environment and prove the
consumer’s needed law, not unconditional global functionality. Term scope alone is not full
certificate parsing.
Thus native source agreement and internal residual execution agreement are both established,
but their internal source-indexed combination is not. A total internal compiler remains

5 Remaining milestones and acceptance gates
1. Computation witnesses. Residual execution laws are now internal. Assemble the
checked comparison cases into arbitrary-trace induction and finish the endpoint laws
for represented lookup and substitution. The proposed joint Zip builder is one possible
implementation, not a prerequisite to every relational route.
2. Finish the internal checker relation. Bind source proof nodes to operations and their
operands, covering all six typing rules, contexts, annotated substitution and supplied
conversion instructions. Derive agreement internally on arbitrary admitted witnesses. Lift
the established standard residual acceptance witnesses to the final raw-code predicate.
3. Prove operational resource sufficiency. Extend the checked storage envelopes to
the actual operations, intermediate states and any proof transport performed. Supply
standard raw-input admission independently of acceptance. Internal compression is
needed only if the chosen resource construction uses compressed labels.
4. Establish internal finite-fragment soundness. Derive a bounded normalizing history,
or a negative continuation serving its purpose, from acceptance and the independent
resources. Preserve a sufficient invariant and internally exclude a normal result at Bottom.
This must generate rejecting branches, not assume an All-package.
5. Assemble uniform bounded soundness. Integrate the dictionary, checker and
resource interfaces at fixed exact ranks and construct (BS) for arbitrary represented
budgets.
6. Eliminate resources and complete adequacy. Construct Consdir, verify its original
formation/typing, and discharge admission, acceptance realization and any translation
to the intended historical sentence.
Resource work and the logical-scope audit can proceed alongside checker agreement and
soundness. They are not reasons to postpone the hard local laws.
5.1 What the soundness milestone still requires
The external normalization proof calls certificate soundness, native normalization/preservation,
and finally NativeCanonicity.normal_void_empty. None of these calls supplies an internal
implication from represented acceptance to a normalizing invariant.
The first substantive preservation issue is annotated dependent substitution: an accepted
application and lambda body must agree on the substituted codomain under their represented
environments, including conversion. The checked substitution graph preserves scope and
realizes standard Syntax.subst; scope alone does not establish that typing/coherence law.
Do not repair a rank mismatch by raising all indices indiscriminately. InvariantObstructions
proves that an implication with domain rank r cannot itself have rank at most r. A same-index
Church induction motive that quantifies over its own higher-rank graph proofs therefore fails.
A whole-history invariant or source-node closure/stack machine may avoid this particular
pattern, but must still prove the binder and conversion laws.
Raw intermediate terms in a valid conversion certificate need not be typed or strongly
normalizing: an accepted certificate containing an untyped beta-loop is formalized. Normalize
the accepted subject, not every supplied conversion intermediate. Use arbitrary-term confluence
or a suitable negative transport law for the latter.

The guarded combinatory development now constructs executable fixed finite models of all six
guarded Hilbert rules, with total operations and actual original-PTS satisfaction certificates
of rank at most four. Their monotone union exactly covers the independent catalogue; finite
proof families share one budget, including every reduction-witness state. This supplies local

6 Simplifications and logical scope
The direct route needs neither H<ω nor an EA derivation. It also does not inherently need
a total budget selector, numerical capacity comparison, global lookup functionality, full
constructor inversion, complete dependent typing reconstruction after every reduction, or a
normal-form output in the admitted domain. Prove the weaker local invariant and coherence
actually required by each consumer. Structural resources can replace numeral conversion.
The PHOAS self-interpreter’s GenericFold supplies typed composition once an algebra is built;
it does not prove that algebra’s source-rule soundness. Decoding a code of Bottom is useful
only after independently obtaining that code. No CPS theorem or rank-only self-interpreter is
a prerequisite here.
Retain the supplied Zermelo revision’s useful devices: complete-certificate rank compression,
independent generation guards, guarded finite elimination, and continuation packaging of
positive resources. Its proof-transport cost claims must be checked for this representation.
From Pakhomov’s construction1, retain the proof-local use of finite resources and the distinction
between possible internal consistency formulations. His transfer theorems are not axioms of
the present development.
The weak local finite satisfiability results in the combinatory note2 are probably highly
relevant to investigating a non-Gödelian interpretation. They do not, by themselves, prove the
required quantitative internal soundness or a failure of a derivability condition. Investigate
Willard’s stability framework3 and its deduction-system dependence4 as a separate bridge: his
self-justifying extensions do not give a proof inside unchanged PTS-rk merely by analogy.
Run that audit now, not only after assembly. Identify the exact domain, proof predicate, avail-
able proof-combination operations and applicable diagonal/derivability conditions. Standard-
instance coverage does not identify two internal consistency sentences; a conventional or
historical claim needs a uniform internal translation. No escape from an applicable incom-
pleteness theorem is asserted.
7 Verification and continuation
Every completed internal construction must expose literal original syntax, formation/typing
derivations, computation laws and fresh transitive assumption reports. Compare complete
recursive kernel inventories with pristine upstream. The separate resource-only counterexample
must not be relabelled a no-go theorem for the entire programme.
