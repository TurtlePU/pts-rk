# Consumers needed by operation provenance

The comparison trace must retain the actual Zip witness as an index. The
comparator executes that witness's fold, so an internal agreement law cannot
silently replace it with some other inhabitant at the same endpoint types.
Local clauses should record the corresponding Zip constructor and Boolean
operation, with no evaluator equation or desired checker answer as admission.

Exact-rank-one Boolean predicate transport from Compare(x,y,z) to a trace's
reported Boolean program is already useful: it instantiates Holds and supports
conditional rejection. It applies to arbitrary represented trace witnesses
if proved by a genuine PTS fold. It is not yet true-comparison-to-endpoint
coherence. In particular the proposed atom-left clause permits arbitrary y
and reports the symbolic root test is_atom(t,y); that clause has no independent
root-generation or inversion evidence. A dependent source consumer may need
additional certified observations of endpoints, not just execution agreement.
Do not call arbitrary Boolean transport raw-code syntactic equality or invoke
unproved Church parametricity to bridge that distinction.

Original context lookup is different from substitution-environment lookup.
Looking up the most recent declaration returns its one-step weakening; crossing
each further declaration introduces another weakening. Its represented local
clauses must retain these exact shifts, use the actual native context encoding
for standard realization, and share the chosen output endpoint with comparison
and source-field consumers. A scope-preservation theorem is useful but does
not alone certify endpoint coherence for arbitrary represented lookup witnesses.

The strongest necessary law is determined by the eventual consumer. Avoid
unconditional functionality for arbitrary replacement environments, which can
be multivalued. Prove the appropriate observation/transport law for the actual
single-substitution or shift environment, or a stronger canonical-output law
if that is easier. Standard quotation correctness must remain separate from
elimination over arbitrary admitted evidence. Neither these operation graphs
nor their scope laws supply internal dependent preservation/terminal exclusion
and hence do not close internal finite-fragment soundness.

This is an early interface review. Final source declarations and the fresh
recursive verification must be inspected before recording checked endpoints.

## Context source review before freeze

Read-only review of ContextLookupCodes, InternalContextLookup and the draft
ContextLookupRealization found no circular target premise. The native literal
context decoder and raw LookupGraph are independent of typing and acceptance.
ContextCode erasure is a native bridge, explicitly not an internal extraction
from certificate serialization. Zero lookup intentionally ignores its prefix;
the independent ContextGuard supplies whole-context scoping when needed.
Both zero lookup and every crossed successor apply the original weakening,
matching Context.index, including annotations. The internal scope consumer
constructs its shift-environment bound from the same successor edge used by
the graph, rather than assuming its desired output scope.

The realization draft constructs standard evidence for every original Ctx
and Fin, not only well-typed contexts, and obtains exactly the original lookup
endpoint. This is appropriate because ContextGuard certifies scoping rather
than declaration inhabitation. Native raw-output uniqueness and standard
realization remain separate from arbitrary represented endpoint coherence.
This review precedes compilation of the realization draft and is not a kernel
verification claim.

and InternalJointTraversal; its realization additionally imports the binary,
scope, renaming-lookup and renaming realization modules. These are absent from
the preceding ninety-endpoint source-provenance build's compiled object set.
The earlier renaming development's complete recursive VM-enabled check took
875 seconds; the latest source-provenance check alone took 452 seconds. These
historical observations motivate the current up-front 3600-second limits,
not an estimate or claim that the expanded closure has already passed.

Worker dependency links are development-only. The final driver creates a
new absolute Dune directory, builds the aggregate from its actual imports,
and maps the recursive kernel checker exclusively to that fresh tree and
the pinned installed libraries. No worker object or earlier build directory
is supplied. The complete inventory is compared with a separately built
pristine original kernel, without skipping old dependencies or admitting
expensive checks. Disk availability rose externally from 150 MiB to 2.3 GiB
at this review; this worker performed no cleanup.

## Guarded finite-model update for the roadmap

The new combinatory results materially improve the alternative route.
GuardedHilbertTables supplies fixed finite positional carriers with total
overflow operations, executable bounded beta/common-reduct graphs, all six
guarded Hilbert rule forms, and all-valuation model laws. Admission checks
syntax (including output bounds) and reduction witnesses, never the desired
typing or consistency. Its finite saturation has no proof-height cutoff.
The public endpoints `semantic_schema_correspondence`,
`every_semantic_valuation`, `semantic_void_empty`, `finite_model`,
`internal_certificate`, `internal_certificate_rank` and `internal_deduction`
include actual original-PTS satisfaction certificates of rank at most four
and compilation of independent fragment deductions. Thus finite-instance
local validity is no longer merely a premise awaiting an instance.

GuardedHilbertCoverage proves `query_monotone`, `executable_union_exact`,
`finite_family_common_budget` and `executable_semantic_coverage`. The last
theorem equates independent CatalogueEconomy derivability with existence of
a successful executable finite-carrier quoted query. Common budgets cover
all displayed syntax and every beta-witness state. They do not establish
original-PTS-to-catalogue completeness, a uniform internal theorem over
represented fragments/proofs, or native/MM0 graph adequacy. Void exclusion
still uses external native canonicity; certificates are externally generated
original-PTS proofs of particular finite-model laws, not reflection of
arbitrary represented model or proof codes. Guarded full55 model existence
also remains separate.
