title: Toward an internal consistency proof for PTS-rk
date: October 22, 2026

:::Abstract
We isolate the internal-consistency argument of the supplied Zermelo-revision
draft and give a formalization plan. Its target is a particular translated,
predicate-only consistency sentence. The principal obligation is an explicit
elementary-arithmetic derivation of soundness under a supplied tower bound.
Existing Rocq normalization and dependent PHOAS results provide reusable
components, but do not yet prove that assertion internally. We compare a route
through Pakhomov's higher-order set theory with a direct interpretation over
a resource-restricted arithmetic domain, and identify the additional benefit
and limits of uniform interpreters for bounded fragments. All proposed new
lemmas below are obligations, not reported formalization results.
:::

# The target and its meaning

Write $T=\mathsf{PTS}_{rk}$. Its annotated terms are variables, sorts $U_i$,
products, abstractions and applications. The characteristic rules are

$$
\Gamma\vdash U_i:U_0,\qquad
\frac{\Gamma\vdash A:U_a\quad\Gamma,x:A\vdash B:U_b}
 {\Gamma\vdash\Pi x:A.B:U_{\max(a+1,b)}}.
$$

Conversion is beta conversion, including annotations. There is no eta rule
or cumulativity. Fix $\bot=\Pi X:U_0.X$, which has type $U_1$.
External consistency is the metatheorem that no closed $t$ has type $\bot$.
An internal consistency result instead constructs a closed original PTS
inhabitant of a specified type representing absence of contradiction proofs.
Its arithmetic domain, equality, coding and proof predicate are part of the
statement.

The supplied draft fixes the beta-only explicit-typed-equality presentation
$T^e$. Besides contexts and typing it has $\Gamma\vdash_e t=u:A$, with
reflexivity, symmetry, transitivity, typed beta, product/abstraction/application
congruence, and equality conversion. Typing conversion consumes an explicit
equality between types at a sort. A binary proof tree records contexts,
annotations, ranks and every premise. Its elementary checker $P(p)$ accepts
exactly certificates deriving a closed inhabitant of $\bot$ and rejects
malformed inputs. This format must still be specified concretely in Rocq.

Choose a predicate-only trace formula

$$
P^{\rm pred}(p)\equiv\exists\vec w\,A(p,\vec w),\qquad
C:=\operatorname{Con}^{\rm pred}(T^e)
  \equiv\forall p\,\forall\vec w\,\neg A(p,\vec w).
$$

Here $A$ has bounded quantifiers with variable bounds; arithmetic operations
are graph predicates with explicit witnesses. Its correctness must give
$\mathsf{EA}\vdash P^{\rm pred}(p)\to P(p)$, where $\mathsf{EA}$ is
elementary arithmetic, with exponentiation and bounded induction. Thus $C$
is a predicate-only $\Pi_1$ sentence. All checker-trace witnesses are quantified;
changing the encoding or replacing this predicate requires a proved comparison.

For the draft's fixed interpretation $J:\mathsf H_{<\omega}\to T$, the goal is

$$
\boxed{\quad\vdash c:J(\mathcal{NAT}(C))\quad}
$$

for an actual annotated PTS term $c$. $\mathcal{NAT}$ uses finite ordinals;
it is not the Church numeral family used for numerical computation. A different
interpretation $K$ gives a potentially different sentence $K(C)$. An external
equivalence between $T^e$ and ordinary PTS does not identify their internal
consistency formulas automatically.

# The arithmetic argument to retain

## Supplied resources and transfer

Put $E_0(x)=x$ and $E_{h+1}(x)=2^{E_h(x)}$. A tower witness is a coded sequence
$w_0=2$, $w_{i+1}=2^{w_i}$, ending at $w_h=B$; abbreviate its existence by
$\operatorname{Tower}(h,2,B)$. Keep the actual sequence as a parameter in
bounded computations. Define

$$
\mathcal S=\{h:\exists B\,\operatorname{Tower}(h,2,B)\},\qquad
I=2\mathcal S=\{p:\lfloor p/2\rfloor\in\mathcal S\}.
$$

Pakhomov starts towers at zero. The two domains agree in EA by the fixed
two-level shift and successor/downward closure; this convention change must
be included in the formal cut comparison.

The draft's central obligation is the following derivability assertion:

$$
\mathsf{EA}\vdash
 P(p)\land\operatorname{Tower}(\lfloor p/2\rfloor,2,B)\to 0=1.
\qquad\text{(BS)}
$$

The free variables are universally closed. With trace correctness and bounded
absoluteness on this downward-closed domain, (BS) implies
$\mathsf{EA}\vdash C^{2\mathcal S}$. Relativization restricts $p$ and every
trace witness. Soundness computations themselves may use ambient witnesses
outside that domain. This asymmetry is useful: consistency is negative and
requires no internally quantified normal-form output.

The exact published bridges are

$$
\begin{aligned}
\mathsf{EA}\vdash\varphi^{\mathcal S}
 &\Longleftrightarrow
 \mathsf H^\omega\vdash\mathcal{NAT}(\varphi)
 &&\text{(Lemma 3.11)},\\
\mathsf{EA}\vdash\varphi^{2\mathcal S}
 &\Longrightarrow
 \mathsf H\vdash\mathcal{NAT}(\varphi)
 &&\text{(Corollary 5.3)},
\end{aligned}
$$

for predicate-only $\Pi_1$ sentences $\varphi$.
These are external dependencies from
[Pakhomov, *A weak set theory that proves its own consistency*, v2](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1907.00877v2).
The draft uses the second bridge. The requested higher-order route can use
the first, since $\mathcal S\subseteq2\mathcal S$ and restriction of a universal
bounded sentence gives $C^{\mathcal S}$. Neither bridge says that interpreting
a theory which proves its own consistency proves consistency of $T$:
the transferred sentence must be this particular $C$.

It suffices to derive the more convenient statement

$$
\mathsf{EA}\vdash
 P(p)\land\operatorname{Tower}(q(\ell(p)),2,B)\to0=1
\qquad\text{(WBS)}
$$

with a fixed polynomial $q$ and binary length $\ell(p)$. Eventually
$q(\ell(p))\le\lfloor p/2\rfloor$, so a prefix of the supplied tower suffices.
Finitely many smaller codes use fixed-height towers, whose existence EA
proves. More generally any elementary height $h(p)$ with that eventual
inequality works; even $2^{\lfloor\ell(p)/2\rfloor}$ is affordable. No padding
of proof codes or total variable-height tower function is needed.

## Six ingredients of the draft construction

The following is the final proposed argument, not a report of kernel-checked
EA derivations. Its retained source labels are listed in the roadmap.

**1. Compress ranks in the supplied certificate.** List all sort indices
occurring anywhere in the proof, adjoin $0,1$, and replace them by their
increasing enumeration $\sigma$. For each used product rule,

$$
\sigma(\max(a+1,b))=\max(\sigma(a)+1,\sigma(b)).
$$

If the result is $a+1$, the two adjacent integers remain adjacent in the
list; if it is $b>a$, strict order suffices. Renaming commutes with
substitution and fixes $\bot$. The largest new index is linear in certificate
length. This gives the polynomial-height estimate despite binary sort labels.

**2. Separate distinct inert constants without normalization.** In an
auxiliary untyped lambda syntax with constants $c_a$, prove in EA

$$
\operatorname{Eq}_\beta(e;c_a,c_b)\to a=b.
$$

The input is a supplied equational proof, not a convertibility decision
problem. The draft transports a finite non-idempotent resource typing of
$c_a$ along its beta zigzag. Resource contexts are multisets and the only
constant axiom types $c_a$ at a ground type. Forward substitution consumes
each supplied argument proof once; inverse substitution extracts disjoint
subderivations at known occurrence origins. Local transport is claimed
polynomial in the fully written data. Iterating it along the supplied proof
has a fixed elementary bound. A final derivation for $c_b$ can only use the
same constant axiom. The formal resource accounting and EA derivation remain
substantial obligations.

**3. Make product compatibility a projection operation.** Decorate products
by their source formation ranks and erase them to auxiliary Church tuples:

$$
\begin{aligned}
\epsilon(\Pi^{a,b}x:A.B)
 &=\langle P,\iota_a,\iota_b,\epsilon(A),\lambda x.\epsilon(B)\rangle,\\
\epsilon(U_i)&=\langle S,\iota_i,\iota_0,d,d\rangle.
\end{aligned}
$$

Here $\langle u_0,\ldots,u_4\rangle:=\lambda k.k\,u_0\cdots u_4$, and
$P,S,d,\iota_0,\iota_1,\ldots$ are pairwise distinct inert constants.
Variables, application and lambda bodies erase homomorphically; lambda
annotations are erased only in this auxiliary map. Fixed tuple projections
turn a supplied equality of product erasures into component equations and,
by constant separation, equality of both rank tags. These coding constants
are never added to PTS. No repeated common-reduct computation is needed.

**4. Lift into a scoped graded analysis calculus.** Its judgment is
$\Gamma\Vdash t:A\,[a]$. Context declarations may refer only to preceding
variables, and every displayed field is scoped. Let $r=\max(a+1,b)$.
The six rules are variable lookup, $U_i:U_0[0]$, and

$$
\begin{gathered}
\Gamma\Vdash\Pi^{a,b}x:A.B:U_r\,[0],\\
\frac{\Gamma,x:A[a]\Vdash t:B[b]}
 {\Gamma\Vdash\lambda x:A.t:\Pi^{a,b}x:A.B[r]},\\
\frac{\Gamma\Vdash f:\Pi^{a,b}x:A.B[r]\quad\Gamma\Vdash u:A[a]}
 {\Gamma\Vdash fu:B[x:=u][b]},\\
\frac{\Gamma\Vdash t:A[a]\quad\operatorname{Eq}_\beta(e;\epsilon(A),\epsilon(B))}
 {\Gamma\Vdash t:B[a]}.
\end{gathered}
$$

The product rule checks scope and numerical tags but needs no formation
premises for its fields. Conversion preserves grade and needs no target
formation proof. This relaxation belongs only to the analysis calculus.

The one-way source lift must construct coherent tags and finite packets of
endpoint typings, type-formation proofs and context data. An untagged tuple
erasure first supports uniqueness of source ranks; typed substitution then
supports tag coherence. Complete packets by recursion over original proof
nodes, using separate structural helpers on generated evidence. Do not
recursively complete every generated regularity proof. The proposed schedule
uses a fixed elementary amount of work per original node, giving a tower
of polynomial height in the original length.

**5. Preserve proofs while reducing only active computation.** Active
positions traverse applications and lambda bodies, never annotations or
product fields. Substitution still updates all fields and their sizes are
charged. Inactive fields cannot be extracted into active code. A redex's
lambda degree is its product grade $\max(a+1,b)$; the binder grade is strictly
smaller and the body-result grade is no larger. Descending degree passes
therefore remove active redexes. A resulting term is weak-head normal.

The critical claim is polynomial construction of each transformed auxiliary
proof: project supplied product equations, convert the argument, substitute
in the body proof, and convert back. Scope, copied labels and declared grades
must survive. If a tower bound $B$ covers initial elaboration, raw computation
and its $K$ steps, the proof sizes satisfy, for one fixed $d$,

$$
s_0\le B,\qquad K\le B,\qquad s_{i+1}\le(s_i+B+2)^d.
$$

Consequently the entire proof history is bounded by a fixed elementary
function of $B$; for example each $s_i$ is bounded by
$E_2(c(B+2))$ for a fixed $c$. Charging a new exponential level per beta step
would instead exceed the available height. Bounded induction with the tower
sequence as parameter is the intended EA verification.

**6. Exclude the terminal judgment.** An alleged closed $t:\bot$ yields
$X:U_0[0]\Vdash tX:X[0]$. No weak-head normal term has this auxiliary judgment.
A lambda requires positive grade. Sorts and products have sort types;
conversion to $X$ contradicts constant separation after substitution for
$X$ and projection. The only possible neutral head is $X$ or a sort/product,
all of grade zero, so none can head a nonempty application spine. The empty
spine $X$ has type $U_0$, also separated from $X$. This finishes (WBS), then
(BS), if the preceding constructions are verified in EA.

The older canonical-certificate obligation, residual confluence-join bounds,
TOWER-hardness and set-model construction are not prerequisites of this final
route. Nor is the manuscript's elementary-function compiler: computing a
checker on standard numerals supplies no universal soundness theorem.

# What the repository already supplies

The useful distinction is between a Rocq metatheorem about PTS, a derivation
in a separately defined arithmetic calculus, and an original PTS proof term.
Moving between these levels requires a proved translation.

- **Normalization and preservation.** `BoundedNormalization.fixed_rank_normalization`
  produces a beta-reachable normal form of the same original type in a
  well-formed context, with cost
  $E_{2d+1}(N+5d+2)$ for semantic redex bound $d$ and node count $N$.
  `Normalization.normalization_in_TOWER` supplies an automatic closed-term
  normalizer; `rank_bound_in_linear_time` supplies the sufficient bound
  $1+\mathsf{maxRankLiteral}(t)+\mathsf{nodes}(t)$. These are checked in the
  documented operation-cost model. Binary serialization, rank compression,
  arithmetic induction and costs of transforming dependent derivations remain
  separate obligations. In particular, the bound counts numerical sort labels
  and cannot silently be read as a polynomial bound in binary input length.
- **Original source evidence.** `FullSource.derivation_iff` relates independent
  finite derivation data to original `Typing.typ`. `DerivationCertificates.derive_sound`
  and `derive_complete` give a certificate checker for that same judgment.
  Their conversion formats differ from $T^e$ and do not yet have its claimed
  elementary binary-certificate bound. `OriginalTypecheck.check_iff` is useful
  for metalevel tests, but calls normalization and is not the proposed
  elementary explicit-proof verifier.
- **Dependent quotation.** The full dependent interpreter provides normal
  PHOAS representations and originally typed unquoters, with literal term/type
  recovery and syntax fidelity. Its endpoints are `every_closed_original`
  and `every_original` in `FullDependentInterpreter`.
  The quoter consumes external derivation data. It is not an
  internal function taking arbitrary arithmetic proof codes.
- **Common fragment ranks.** `CappedDependentInterpreter.every_bounded_original`
  fixes a source quotation budget $k$ before choosing the source; the
  interpreter's type-sort index is at most $\max(k+7,r+6)$, where $r$ is the
  syntax-tag carrier rank. `fragments_cover` covers all closed original
  judgments. The numerical allocation plan still determines the interpreter
  term and its signature. A type-sort bound alone is not a bound on every
  redex degree in its implementation.
- **Internal logical infrastructure.** The arithmetic interpretation provides
  annotated builders, negative classical logic, equality transport, finite
  observations and independent source deduction systems.
  `FunctionalDeduction.sentence_preservation` and `Theorem7.effective_deduction`
  construct original PTS witnesses for derivable $R$-sentences. Neither is
  an interpretation of EA or of $\mathsf H^\omega$. The effective witness
  search is not the still-unformalized elementary compiler.

The set-model libraries offer examples of keeping native agreement separate
from internal deductions. Their supplied hierarchy and choice assumptions,
and the unfinished material BZC soundness reduction, do not yield this internal
PTS result. They are unnecessary dependencies for the proposed syntactic route.

A useful first new theorem is purely syntactic: no normal $u$ has type $X$
in the original context $X:U_0$. Prove it by inversion and neutral-head
classification, independently of normalization. Existing normalization then
gives a short external consistency baseline. Arithmetizing this proof may
replace parts of the graded route, but only if its invariants and bounds are
verified in EA; a native Rocq proof cannot be imported as an arithmetic axiom.

# How much would uniform PHOAS help?

There are three distinct uniformities: a common numerical rank bound; one
interpreter term and fixed interface for every source in a fragment; and an
internal operation quantifying over represented proof codes and budgets.
The capped construction establishes the first. The second is open; the third
is additional work even if the second succeeds.

For a fixed external $k$, a useful internal interface would be

$$
e_k:\operatorname{Code}_k(\bot)\to\bot,\qquad
q_k:\Pi p:D.\operatorname{Accepted}_k(p)\to\operatorname{Code}_k(\bot).
$$

These are proposed original PTS types, with $D$, acceptance, code type and
their formation ranks specified independently. Constructing both gives a term
$\lambda p\,a.e_k(q_k\,p\,a)$ refuting accepted contradiction certificates in
that fragment. This uses typed unquotation directly; it need not normalize
the decoded inhabitant. The hard new obligation is $q_k$: internal parsing,
typing-certificate verification, reification and identification of the
represented conclusion with literal $\bot$ or a proved internal equality to it.
Acceptance must express $K$'s fixed checker predicate, with all trace
witnesses, and independently defined fragment membership.
In particular, prove internally that $K(A(p,\vec w))$ together with those
fragment bounds implies $\operatorname{Accepted}_k(p)$ for arbitrary admitted
$p,\vec w$.
Quotation from a supplied Rocq derivation
does not construct this term. A shallow evaluator into a merely represented
proposition instead requires a separate soundness/falsehood bridge.

Uniform fragment interpreters could remove changing signatures and per-proof
plans from that interface, simplify its rank accounting, and make a proof of
internal reification reusable for arbitrarily large syntax. They do not
automatically supply an admissible arithmetic domain or a single term ranging
over all external $k$. Sort indices are syntax, not internal natural-number
parameters. An external family of fragment-consistency proofs is not one
proof of the unbounded sentence.

Even the intended fragment must be specified carefully. The capped budget
bounds pruned source formation/literal data; its relation to a semantic
redex-rank bound is open. All $U_i:U_0$ have low result rank, so that rank
alone leaves the literal alphabet unbounded. `LiteralObstruction.no_uniform_sort_unquoter`
limits literal recovery with a fixed head and uniformly bounded input literals.
It permits interfaces whose ordinary arguments already carry the necessary
literals. `PlanNonuniformity` refutes rank-only bounds for the present
allocation algorithm, and `FiniteCoherenceCatalogue.no_finite_literal_catalogue`
refutes exhaustive finite lists of concrete fields. Neither rules out
generic coherence laws or represented environments.

The most economical direct route may consequently use a resource-relativized
proof-code evaluator specialized to contradictions, rather than solve general
fragment-uniform strong unquotation first. The draft's negative target allows
this choice. It must still establish universal internal acceptance soundness;
testing standard codes or giving an externally total program is insufficient.
Such an evaluator need not retain a raw syntax tag, syntax injectivity or
literal source-term recovery. `DependentProgramDecoder.decoder_typed` and
`evaluate_typed` already isolate typed evaluation before the faithful tag is
added. Reusing this smaller interface still requires the internal connection
between the represented result and $\bot$.

# Two routes to formalization

## Route A: interpret the higher-order theory, then use external transfer

The draft's $\mathsf H_{<\omega}$ and the requested
$\mathsf H^\omega_{<\omega}$ differ. The latter adds finite-order classes,
extensionality and comprehension to the material theory with extensionality,
separation and a hierarchy operation $V$. Its defining material equation is

$$
y\in V(x)\ \longleftrightarrow\ (\exists z\in x)\ y\subseteq V(z).
$$

The $<\omega$ extension asserts existence of each individual finite ordinal.
$\mathcal{NAT}$ is the finite-ordinal predicate arithmetic, with potentially
partial successor and other operations. Appendix A's mutual interpretation
with $R$ concerns the first-order theory; it does not already supply the
higher-order interpretation. For the target $C$, interpreting $\mathsf H^\omega$
alone suffices by Lemma 3.11. These distinctions follow from
[Pakhomov's definitions and transfer results](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1907.00877v2).

More explicitly, order $0$ contains material objects and order $n+1$ classes
of order-$n$ objects, with separate membership $\varepsilon_n$. At each class
order impose extensionality and comprehension

$$
\exists X^{n+1}\,\forall y^n\,
 (y\varepsilon_n X\leftrightarrow\varphi(y)),
$$

where $\varphi$ is any finite-order formula with parameters and $X$ is not
free in it. Material extensionality uses $\in$; class separation is

$$
\forall x\,\forall Y^1\,\exists z\,\forall w\,
 [w\in z\leftrightarrow(w\in x\land w\varepsilon_0Y)].
$$

Construct the requested direct interpretation $J_\omega$ in four stages.

1. Define an independent finite-order source syntax and deduction calculus,
   including equality, class membership, material membership, $V$, and the
   precise comprehension schemes. Encode $\mathcal{NAT}$ and the particular
   sentence $C$ in it. Do not restrict source comprehension to formulas which
   the chosen target happens to represent easily.
2. Supply original PTS carriers, domain predicates and extensional equality
   relations for material objects and every finite class order. Reuse the
   negative logic and equality-transport infrastructure. An observational
   finite-set representation is a candidate, not an established model;
   separation, the $V$ equation and higher-order comprehension need all-index
   realization proofs. Equality may be interpreted by a relation and need
   not coincide with beta conversion.
3. Prove substitution, formation, every source axiom instance and deduction
   preservation. The immediate design test is comprehension: a formula
   defining an order-$n$ class can quantify at arbitrarily higher orders.
   The naive choice $D_{n+1}=D_n\to\mathsf{Prop}_r$ does not provide a fixed
   rank for all these formulas. Finite-proof rank allocation may suffice for
   translating a particular proof, but must produce the same final target,
   or exhibit explicit comparison maps. It is not automatically a single
   interpretation of the whole theory. No general impossibility is inferred.
4. Combine a checked EA derivation of (BS) with the external Lemma 3.11, then
   translate the resulting $\mathsf H^\omega$ derivation to obtain
   $\vdash c:J_\omega(\mathcal{NAT}(C))$. The first-order fallback interprets
   $\mathsf H$ and uses Corollary 5.3; it avoids higher-order comprehension.

Keep the external boundary explicit in Rocq. Prove a generic translator taking
an independently defined source derivation, and separately construct the EA
derivation of bounded soundness. While Pakhomov's theorem is left unverified,
the existence of the intervening derivation remains a paper-level inference;
there is no closed kernel-checked final witness from that citation alone.
Alternatively, supply a concrete finite $\mathsf H^\omega$ proof certificate
and check it, without formalizing the general transfer theorem. Do not add an
axiom or a hypothesis asserting the desired PTS consistency result.

## Route B: directly interpret the resource-restricted proof arithmetic

Define a fixed interpretation $K$ of the needed predicate-only proof-code
language in PTS, with independently specified domain $D$. Its admission
criterion may express finite resource certificates or an appropriate tower
cut; it must not assert checker rejection or the desired soundness theorem.
This is an interpretation of the required restricted arithmetic and proof
reasoning, not a claim that PTS interprets unrestricted EA.

1. Construct numerical codes, graph predicates, bounded trace reasoning and
   the domain predicate. Prove admission of every standard numeral, required
   initial-segment properties, and exactly the closure/partial-totality laws
   used by checking. Define $K(C)$ before proving it. A domain chosen merely
   to omit counterexamples would not address the intended consistency claim.
2. Prove internal soundness for arbitrary admitted certificates. One option
   internalizes the draft's finite resource and graded-proof argument directly.
   Another uses the $q_k,e_k$ interface above, with independently implemented
   checker-to-code reification and proof of its original typing. A third is a
   bounded normalizer plus internal preservation and terminal impossibility.
   Compare these at the same certificate interface; none may assume external
   Rocq normalization inside the object logic.
3. Close the gap between a family indexed externally and a single formula.
   Represent sufficient budgets inside the chosen domain, and derive the
   ability to reason with them. Existence of a fixed resource certificate
   must entail enough resources for the actual input, with verified bounds.
   If the implementation has only $e_k$ for each external $k$, state the
   result as a family until a genuine internal packaging/relativization
   theorem is constructed. Replacing the plan by one encoded number does
   not solve this issue.
4. Construct a closed term of $K(C)$. Compare $K$ with the requested finite-
   ordinal interpretation if the exact draft sentence is required. External
   agreement on standard numerals is insufficient: domain and predicate
   correspondence, and the direction needed to transfer consistency, must
   themselves be proved internally.

This route omits material sets and the external transfer theorem, but takes
on their arithmetic-domain and reflection work directly. Uniform PHOAS could
make stage 2 smaller. It does not remove stages 1, 3 or 4. It is a promising
research branch, not yet a completed alternative proof.

# Dependency order and concrete milestones

Reserve the namespace `PTSrk.SelfConsistency` and add modules only with actual
definitions and proofs. The following order keeps the two routes comparable.

1. **Freeze the statement.** `ExplicitPTS` and `CertificateCoding` define the
   exact independent $T^e$ calculus, binary serialization, elementary checker
   and trace predicate. Prove acceptance-to-original-typing and scope soundness;
   prove completeness against the chosen source calculus. No unproved
   identification with existing certificate formats. `NormalBottom` proves
   the original normal-form obstruction and external consistency baseline.
2. **Make arithmetic derivability explicit.** `EASyntax`, `EADeduction`,
   `BoundedTraces`, `TowerCut` and `RankCompression` specify actual EA proofs,
   trace encodings and witnessed resources. Build the source-to-unary-size
   bridge after compression. First acceptance test: an EA proof of the
   adequacy of a fixed polynomial height, including the finite exceptions.
3. **Verify the highest-risk draft lemmas.** `ResourceSeparation` proves
   `lem:ea-constant-separation`, including the inverse-substitution size bound.
   `TaggedProjection` gives `lem:tagged-projection`. `GradedCalculus` and
   `GradedTransport` give `lem:structural-templates` and `lem:aux-one-step`.
   `SourcePackets` handles `lem:source-tag-coherence` and `lem:source-elaboration`
   with a finite dependency graph. Reject any design requiring unrestricted
   regularity completion or repeated normalization of residual equalities.
4. **Finish witnessed soundness.** Prove `lem:active-passes` in `ActivePasses`
   and `lem:aux-terminal` in `TerminalObstruction`; assemble `thm:bs-proved`
   in `BoundedSoundness`. The endpoint must be an inhabitant of the independent
   EA-derivation type for (WBS)/(BS), not merely a Rocq proposition about
   standard naturals. Track whole proof-history sizes, not only term sizes.
   Replace a component by existing normalization infrastructure only after
   proving the same internal and quantitative contract.
5. **Test both routes early.** Start these tests after milestone 2, in parallel
   with the draft lemmas. Route A's module `HigherOrderInterpretation` must
   realize a comprehension instance with
   quantified order above the class being defined and a stable target rank
   policy. For Route B, `InternalReification` must construct the fixed-fragment
   $q_k$ over arbitrary admitted codes. Success on closed examples is not
   either test. Use these outcomes to choose the shorter final route.
6. **Assemble and audit.** Route A records the precise external transfer
   dependency or checks a supplied transferred proof. Route B supplies
   `ResourceDomain`, internal budget packaging and `DirectConsistency`.
   Print transitive assumptions and original typing of every final PTS term;
   check source deduction independently, with no new axioms, admissions,
   changed typing rules or unsafe flags. Register a Dune audit only when
   actual proof modules exist. Use fresh scoped build directories for fresh
   assumption output and remove completed caches after retaining logs.

The near-term recommendation is to share milestones 1--2, then prioritize
resource separation and polynomial proof transport. They test the draft's
most consequential quantitative claims. Higher-order comprehension and
internal reification are independent early feasibility experiments; developing
a general uniform self-interpreter need not block the narrower consistency
argument.

# Scope and provenance

Uniform internal strong normalization is a different target: its $\forall d\exists c$
reduction-tree statement requires witnesses inside the interpreted domain.
(BS) supplies no such closure. The draft's finite-level models refute unrestricted
$\Pi_2$ transfer already for $\forall x\exists y\,x<y$.

The specific arithmetic interpretation and proof predicate also matter for
Gödel's second incompleteness theorem. Their standard self-provability
derivability conditions have not been established; the known $R$ interpretation
neither supplies them nor excludes interpretations of stronger arithmetic.

The source is the supplied Zermelo revision, `sec:internal-consistency`.
Its argument and finite experiments are not a formal proof. `EVIDENCE.md`
records source identity, repository declarations and document verification.
