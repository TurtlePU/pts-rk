title: A direct route to internal consistency of PTS-rk
date: October 24, 2026

:::Abstract
The goal is an actual original-PTS inhabitant of a resource-relativized
consistency statement, without using $H_{<\omega}$ or Elementary Arithmetic
as intermediate proof theories. The development already supplies faithful
certificate codes, source-rank compression, an internal rejection proof for
every externally bounded raw-code fragment, and fixed internal dictionary
and comparison operations. Independent local source graphs now prove
source-to-operation agreement externally, and one fixed internal term proves
residual execution agreement for arbitrary represented execution witnesses.
The combinatory development supplies internal soundness and proof transport
for arbitrary inhabitants of independent indexed Horn proof types. A generic
Horn family now binds fragment and clause data internally; a rank-two-type
finite table returns faithful raw syntax without generation evidence.
Neither constructs the source-rule interpretation for arbitrary represented
budgets. That bridge, with source, endpoint and resource provenance, remains
open. Reusing Horn elimination can replace a separate internal
normalization/preservation argument.
It is not a claim of internal consistency.
:::

# Target and proof obligations

Use the original annotated syntax, $\mathsf{Typing.typ}$, and
$\mathsf{Ranks.RankSorts}$ throughout. In particular,

$$
 U_i:U_0,\qquad
 \mathsf{rank}(\Pi x:A.B)=\max(a+1,b)
 \quad(A:U_a,\ B:U_b),\qquad
 \bot=\Pi X:U_0.X:U_1.
$$

There is no cumulativity, eta rule, new primitive, or assumed soundness
principle. A Rocq theorem that no native term inhabits $\bot$ is an
external result; the target here is a closed PTS term of a specified
consistency type.

Let $D$ be a fixed carrier of complete proof codes and $B$ a fixed carrier
of resources. Codes include contexts, annotations, binary ranks, conversion
instructions and any supplied computation witnesses. Define independently:

- $\mathsf{Acc}_{\bot}(q)$: the source certificate passes its local rule
  checks and concludes a closed term at the literal syntactic type $\bot$;
- $\mathsf{Res}(q,b)$: structural generation, envelopes and the operation
  capacities needed for the proof.

Acceptance must not contain native typing or semantic soundness as a field.
Resources must not assert rejection, validity of a source model, or existence
of a normalizing history. These are conclusions to derive. Standard rejected
and malformed codes must also receive resources.

The main remaining theorem is an actual term

$$
 \mathsf{bs}:
 \Pi q:D.\Pi b:B.\,
 \mathsf{Res}(q,b)\to\mathsf{Acc}_{\bot}(q)\to\bot.
 \qquad\text{(BS)}
$$

All its carriers and quantified types must use one fixed finite collection
of ranks, independently of represented source ranks, proof lengths and budgets.

## Final resource elimination

The following continuation package avoids demanding a uniform budget selector:

$$
\begin{aligned}
 \mathsf{Adm}(q)
   &:=\Pi Z:U_1.\,
       (\Pi b:B.\mathsf{Res}(q,b)\to Z)\to Z,\\
 \mathsf{Cons}_{\mathrm{dir}}
   &:=\Pi q:D.\mathsf{Adm}(q)\to\mathsf{Acc}_{\bot}(q)\to\bot.
\end{aligned}
$$

Once (BS) is constructed, the proposed final term is

$$
 \lambda q\,a\,h.\;a\,\bot\,(\lambda b\,r.\mathsf{bs}\,q\,b\,r\,h).
$$

Explicit resources introduce admission by $\lambda Z\,k.k\,b\,r$.
For $D:U_d$, $B:U_e$, $\mathsf{Res}(q,b):U_r$ and
$\mathsf{Acc}_{\bot}(q):U_s$, the calculated formation ranks are
$\max(e+2,r+2)$ for $\mathsf{Adm}(q)$ and
$\max(d+1,e+3,r+3,s+1)$ for $\mathsf{Cons}_{\mathrm{dir}}$.
These final builders and their original formation derivations remain
obligations, not consequences of the displayed notation.

Adequacy has three separate requirements: admission of every standard raw
code, actual internal acceptance witnesses for every accepted standard
judgment, and completeness of the source certificates for original typing.
The third is already proved externally. The first two must not be obtained
by eliminating the externally impossible Bottom case.

# Completed foundations and their exact scope

The library `PTSrk.SelfConsistency` imports the original kernel and shared
annotated assembler/checker, not an arithmetic interpretation or set model.
Its detailed module inventory is in [README.md](README.md); verification
commands and assumptions are in [EVIDENCE.md](EVIDENCE.md).

- **Certificates and serialization:** an independent six-rule checker,
  original-typing adequacy and faithful annotated raw codes, proved externally.
- **Rank compression:** complete inputs are relabeled; checking and Bottom
  are preserved; labels are bounded by serialized size. This is not internal
  all-source soundness.
- **Syntactic operations:** fixed-rank lookup, histories, scope, renaming and
  annotated substitution relations with standard witnesses. Scope is not
  dependent typing preservation.
- **Finite Bottom rejection:** rank-four internal rejection at every external
  complete-code size bound, as an externally generated schema.
- **Dictionaries:** one rank-five-type polymorphic consumer for represented
  lists and independent membership evidence. Rejecting branch packages are
  still externally supplied.
- **Residual execution:** actual structural comparison and conjunction with
  exact native-checker output on compiled standard inputs. Source compilation
  remains external.

## Why the finite schema is already a genuine internal result

For an external bound $b$, enumerate all raw codes

$$
 Q_b=\{q\mid\mathsf{codeSize}(q)\le b\}.
$$

Admission does not test parsing or acceptance. The size counts complete
certificates, including annotations, binary fields and conversion witnesses.
Decoding this finite family supplies one conservative rank, subject-size
bound and finite label alphabet. The improved combinatory normalization
results then cover every state along the chosen normalization of every
decoded accepted closed subject in a common finite syntactic carrier,
retaining the original concluding type.

Those normalization and canonicity arguments are **external Rocq proofs**.
Their rejection equations certify a finite table of the unchanged Bottom
checker `raw_bottom_check`. For selectors $e_i:E_b$ naming all entries
and this Bottom-checker table $f_b$, an independent
finite-case guard is

$$
 \mathsf{Can}_b(x)=
 \Pi P:E_b\to U_2.\,P(e_0)\to\cdots\to P(e_{k-1})\to P(x):U_3.
$$

With $\mathsf{Holds}(v)=v\,U_1\,\top\,\bot$, each branch of
$P(x)=\mathsf{Holds}(f_bx)\to\bot$ converts to $\bot\to\bot$.
Finite elimination gives an actual original-PTS term

$$
 c_b:\Pi x:E_b.\mathsf{Can}_b(x)\to
             \mathsf{Holds}(f_bx)\to\bot,
 \qquad\mathsf{type}(c_b):U_4.
$$

This handles arbitrary guarded inputs, not just canonical selectors.
Quotation remains beta-injective at each bound. However, the carrier,
table and branch proof are chosen externally from $b$.

Represented Church dictionaries remove the varying case telescope from the
consumer: a separate All-package holds branch proofs, while Guard/Fill
express independent membership and unused-branch filling. One internal
eliminator consumes them. It does not construct All from an arbitrary
represented budget. In particular, the identity cannot inhabit
$\mathsf{Holds}(fx)\to\bot$ for symbolic $f,x$ merely by unfolding a fold.
The implemented dictionary parameter is $E:U_1$; integration with the raw
carrier $C:U_2$ still needs an exact-rank adaptation.

`FiniteRawTables` supplies a simpler nondependent bridge:

$$
 d_b:E_b\to C,\qquad\mathsf{type}(d_b):U_2.
$$

The term re-abstracts the raw carrier's algebra and instantiates the finite
selector only at its final $X:U_0$, never at $C:U_2$. It accepts arbitrary
open bare selectors. Canonical inputs reduce forward to algebra-expanded
quotations, proved beta-equivalent to the original quotations. Expanded
quotation remains syntax-injective. These are beta laws for explicit
constructors, not eta or parametricity for arbitrary inhabitants.

The separate `FiniteRawDecoding` route consumes independent
$\mathsf{Enum}_2(x)$, the finite generation predicate above. Its constant
motive $\lambda x.C$ gives a rank-four-type dependent eliminator whose
canonical outputs reduce forward to the literal original quotations.
Every raw tree, including malformed inputs, receives typed admission at its
serialized size, with the native complete-input decoding roundtrip retained.
The two decoders agree on canonical witnesses. Neither internally parses
complete inputs, proves arbitrary-output coherence, or constructs its finite
dictionary from an arbitrary represented budget.

# Internal computation and represented resources

## Comparisons retain their operands

`CheckerComparisons` compiles the unchanged checker into

$$
 e::=\mathsf{Truth}(b)\mid\mathsf{Compare}(q,r)\mid\mathsf{Both}(e,e').
$$

It covers all six rules, contexts, conversion instructions and malformed
inputs. Comparison leaves contain full raw operands, not precomputed
equality answers. Parsing, context lookup and computing endpoints, including
substitution, currently occur during this external compilation.

`InternalCodeComparison` provides an independent traversal
$\mathsf{Zip}(x,y):U_4$ over the raw carrier $C:U_2$. Its clauses describe
an atom versus arbitrary input, a pair versus an atom, and two pairs with
corresponding child traversals. None contains a Boolean answer or rejection
claim. One comparator of full-type rank five folds these clauses into
actual tag tests and conjunctions. Its root test opens the Boolean answer
type $X:U_0$ before folding $C$; it does not treat $\mathsf{Bool}:U_1$
as an admissible $U_0$ fold argument.

A rank-three conjunction-expression carrier and a fixed evaluator of
full-type rank four execute those comparisons. On the externally compiled
standard input, `CheckerExecution` proves exact forward reduction:

$$
 \mathsf{run}(\mathsf{compile}(q))
       \twoheadrightarrow_\beta\mathsf{point}(b)
 \quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad
 \mathsf{rawCheck}(q)=b.
$$

The Bottom checker has the analogous theorem. Both Boolean outcomes are
retained. Raw serialization/quotation is faithful, but residual checking
programs need not be: different sort certificates can yield the same program.

## Joint traversal: checked interface and remaining construction

`InternalJointTraversal` represents aligned operands in a rank-four carrier,
with actual raw projections and an independent rank-six generation guard.
Its constructors and formation are checked. The proposed rank-seven-type
operation turning guarded joint data into $\mathsf{Zip}$ evidence is not:
large conversion checks exhausted resources. This is a proof-engineering
limit, not a no-go theorem. Even that operation would leave alignment of
independent inputs and source agreement to prove. The Horn route does not
require this particular builder.

## Storage bounds now include full operands

`CheckerResourceBounds` records both complete operands at every synchronized
comparison node. For its raw traversal data $T(q,r)$,

$$
 |T(q,r)|\le(3+|q|+|r|)\min(|q|,|r|).
$$

The residual-data encoding retains every expression constructor and operand;
it is injective even though the executed program need not be. Exact size
accounting and original-PTS capacity witnesses cover the source, expression
data, every comparison operand and every recursive traversal payload.
One externally constructed envelope covers a whole complete-code
size-bounded fragment, including malformed and rejected sources.

These are **storage** results, not bounds on emitted PTS terms, reduction
costs, or the compiler's intermediate allocations. The envelope itself is
still generated externally. It does not assert that the stored expression
is the checker program belonging to the stored source.

# Source provenance and internal execution

## Independent source graphs

Resource coverage does not determine a program's meaning. In
`ComputationBoundary`, a scoped rejected source $q$ has actual generation,
capacity and traversal witnesses, while $\mathsf{Compare}(q,q)$ returns true.
This refutes the resource-only agreement contract in Rocq, not the whole
consistency programme or an internally quoted universal sentence.

`CheckerSourceGraph` repairs this contract **at the native level**. Its local
inductive clauses record source fields, beta/equality endpoints, context
lookup, all six typing-rule checks, context checks and both raw wrappers.
Matching nodes retain the two actual operands and conjunction topology.
No clause assumes acceptance, native typing or evaluator/checker agreement.
Induction proves agreement with the unchanged compiler, uniqueness,
coverage of every raw source, and both Boolean outcomes. Consequently,
adding this graph to the independently generated storage package excludes
the self-comparison counterexample.

Original substitution, context lookup, encoding and decoding in these
clauses are still native operations. This graph is a specification with
proved standard coverage, not yet its internal PTS representation.

## One internal execution-agreement term

For the existing expression carrier $E:U_3$ and $\mathsf{Bool}:U_1$,
`InternalExecutionGraph` independently represents the local clauses

$$
 \mathsf{Run}(\mathsf{leaf}(b),b),\qquad
 \frac{\mathsf{Run}(e,b)\quad\mathsf{Run}(f,c)}
 {\mathsf{Run}(\mathsf{both}(e,f),\mathsf{and}(b,c))}.
$$

Here $b,c$ are arbitrary Boolean programs, not necessarily canonical
selectors. The clauses contain no evaluator equation. A Church fold with
exact motive rank three gives $\mathsf{Run}(e,b):U_5$. Define

$$
 \mathsf{Same}(b,c):=
 \Pi P:\mathsf{Bool}\to U_1.\,P(b)\to P(c):U_3.
$$

The checked original-PTS term

$$
 \mathsf{agree}:\Pi e:E.\Pi b:\mathsf{Bool}.\,
 \mathsf{Run}(e,b)\to\mathsf{Same}(\mathsf{evaluate}(e),b)
$$

has full-type rank six and applies to **arbitrary represented execution
witnesses**. Its proof folds the local clauses, using the evaluator's beta
laws and predicate transport through conjunction. This is exact-rank
Leibniz transport, not arbitrary-rank equality or beta equality.

Taking $P=\mathsf{Holds}$ yields an internal acceptance-transport term and
one rank-six-type rejection operation

$$
 \Pi e:E.\,\mathsf{Run}(e,\mathsf{false})\to
       \mathsf{Holds}(\mathsf{evaluate}(e))\to\bot.
$$

False execution must first be derived; it is not resource admission and
does not itself establish source rejection.

`CheckerGraphRealization` constructs execution witnesses by recursion on
residual syntax. Their outputs are the symbolic conjunctions of actual
comparison programs, not precomputed checker answers. Applying the single
internal agreement term and the native source graph gives exact standard
realizations for both outputs. Ordinary accepted judgments receive actual
inhabitants of residual acceptance; false outputs receive conditional
rejection inhabitants. No external Bottom-emptiness argument is used.
Raw quotation remains faithful; neither residual programs nor these
execution witnesses are claimed to encode sources injectively.

## Represented operations and the remaining boundary

Original context lookup now has a separate represented operation graph.
Literal contexts are encoded by $\mathsf{empty}$ and
$\mathsf{ext}(g,A)$; these are not certificate-context codes, which also
store declaration proofs and ranks. The native erasure between them is
explicit. The lookup clauses perform one annotated shift at **each**
crossed extension, including index zero:

$$
 \frac{\mathsf{Shift}_{n,n+1}(A,A')}
 {\mathsf{Lookup}(n+1,\mathsf{ext}(g,A),0,A')},\qquad
 \frac{\mathsf{Lookup}(n,g,i,A)\quad\mathsf{Shift}_{n,n+1}(A,A')}
 {\mathsf{Lookup}(n+1,\mathsf{ext}(g,B),i+1,A')}.
$$

The displayed successors abbreviate explicit represented successor edges.
Lookup fibers have rank 18; a rank-19-type term derives output scope from
an arbitrary lookup witness. Whole-context scoping is a separate rank-14
guard: the zero clause need not inspect its prefix. Executable standard
witnesses follow the original `Context.index` exactly and retain injective
context quotation. These facts do not prove arbitrary-output functionality,
dependent typing, or internal certificate-context erasure.

For comparison, a proof-indexed relation
$\mathsf{Trace}(x,y,z,b):U_6$ retains the **actual** witness
$z:\mathsf{Zip}(x,y)$. Its local clauses specify the corresponding Zip
constructor and symbolic tag test or conjunction, not evaluator agreement.
Formation and all three local Boolean-agreement cases are checked for
arbitrary open inputs, with child agreement as induction data. The full
constructor/induction assembly and its intended agreement term remain
unverified. A separate executable symbolic-output compiler has the correct
beta law for every raw operand pair, including malformed trees. Standard
beta agreement is not a substitute for the missing arbitrary-trace theorem.

The represented source graph should retain context, certificate, subject,
type and expression indices, sharing each chosen endpoint across its uses.
Most endpoint clauses only assemble syntax. The substantive operations are:

- context lookup's endpoint law and its connection to certificate-context
  erasure, beyond the proved standard witnesses and output-scope law;
- annotated substitution for application and beta-contraction endpoints,
  with the coherence needed by their comparison consumers;
- product-rank computation and complete certificate/context parsing,
  including malformed failures.

Existing replacement evidence proves arbitrary-output scope preservation
and realizes the exact standard substitution. It does not prove arbitrary
endpoint coherence; its general environment can be multivalued. Restrict to
the actual single-substitution environment and prove the consumer's needed
law, not unconditional global functionality. Term scope alone is not full
certificate parsing.

Thus native source agreement and internal residual execution agreement are
both established, but their **internal source-indexed combination** is not.
A total internal compiler remains optional. Independently specified local
relational traces suffice only after internal agreement and standard
coverage are proved; inserting evaluator/checker agreement as a field
would still assume the missing theorem.

# Remaining milestones and acceptance gates

1. **Computation witnesses.** Residual execution laws are now internal.
   Prove the endpoint laws actually used by the source bridge. On the current
   checker route this includes arbitrary comparison-trace agreement and
   lookup/substitution coherence. Neither that particular implementation
   nor the proposed joint Zip builder is an unconditional prerequisite.
2. **Internal source bridge.** Translate independently admitted source proof
   nodes into the guarded Horn proof type, or finish the direct checker
   relation. Cover all six original typing rules, contexts, annotated
   substitution and supplied conversion instructions. Establish internal
   agreement and standard acceptance without inserting them as fields.
3. **Prove operational resource sufficiency.** Extend the checked storage
   envelopes to the actual operations, intermediate states and any proof
   transport performed. Supply standard raw-input admission independently
   of acceptance. Internal compression is needed only if the chosen
   resource construction uses compressed labels.
4. **Internal finite-fragment soundness.** Reuse the checked arbitrary-proof
   Horn consumer after the source bridge, with the required fragment and
   false-query interface. Alternatively derive bounded normalization or a
   sufficient negative continuation and exclude Bottom internally. Generate
   rejecting branches; do not assume an All-package.
5. **Assemble uniform bounded soundness.** Integrate the dictionary,
   checker and resource interfaces at fixed exact ranks and construct (BS)
   for arbitrary represented budgets.
6. **Eliminate resources and complete adequacy.** Construct
   $\mathsf{Cons}_{\mathrm{dir}}$, verify its original formation/typing,
   and discharge admission, acceptance realization and any translation to
   the intended historical sentence.

Resource work and the logical-scope audit can proceed alongside checker
agreement and soundness. They are not reasons to postpone the hard local laws.

## Reuse indexed Horn soundness

The combinatory development has finite total models of all six Hilbert rules
and all 55 guarded MM0 axioms at size $N\ge5$, depth $d\ge3$, with unguarded
Void denial. It now goes beyond standard satisfaction certificates:
`IndexedHornSoundness` and `MM0IndexedReflection` provide actual original-PTS
consumers for **arbitrary open queries and proof inhabitants**. Independent
$\mathsf{Proof}(q):U_3$ quantifies a predicate with rank-one fibers over the finite atom
carrier and the positive clause handlers; it contains no model or
source-typing premise. Consumer types have rank four, and the fixed-MM0
positive model premise is discharged. `IndexedHornTransport` maps proofs
along literal atom maps with proved clause inclusion. `MM0InternalDecoder`
instantiates this with semantic-to-raw observations. Supports are fixed
before the query, not chosen from it.

Reuse this consumer instead of rebuilding internal dependent preservation
when a source-to-Horn bridge suffices. The outstanding bridge must handle
arbitrary admitted original-PTS proof codes, not just externally compiled
proofs. Exact union coverage currently concerns the independent catalogue;
original-PTS-to-catalogue completeness remains open. Queries are now internal;
finite clause tables, supports and budgets remain external parameters. The
Void specialization also retains
external native canonicity in its certification. Thus the fixed-fragment
Horn core is checked, but milestone 4 for the original raw-source interface
and an independent internal consistency proof are not complete.

## A uniform clause interface, not yet a uniform model

`UniformHorn` removes the externally varying clause telescope from the
generic proof algebra. Its actual PTS arguments are $D:U_1$, $A:U_a$
($a=1$ or $2$), families $\mathsf{Cl}(d):U_1$ and
$\mathsf{Pos}(d,c):U_1$, and projections
$\mathsf{prem}(d,c,i),\mathsf{head}(d,c):A$. Define

$$
\begin{aligned}
 \mathsf{Prem}_X(d,c)&:=\Pi i:\mathsf{Pos}(d,c).X(\mathsf{prem}(d,c,i)):U_2,\\
 \mathsf{Handler}_X(d)&:=\Pi c:\mathsf{Cl}(d).
    \mathsf{Prem}_X(d,c)\to X(\mathsf{head}(d,c)):U_3,\\
 \mathsf{Proof}(d,q)&:=\Pi X:A\to U_1.
    \mathsf{Handler}_X(d)\to X(q):U_4.
\end{aligned}
$$

One constructor combines represented child proofs. One consumer sends
$p$ to $p\,V\,h_V$; both have full-type rank five and a checked fold beta law.
The fragment $d$, query, proof and all projection functions are genuine
internal arguments. The two exact atom ranks are checked separately, not
identified by cumulativity. The proof family contains no semantic premise;
the consumer's interpretation $V$ and handler $h_V$ are supplied separately.

`UniformHornReindex` also binds a clause family $S(d):U_1$ and a map
$g_d:S(d)\to\mathsf{Cl}(d)$ internally. Source positions, premises and heads
are literal pullbacks along $g_d$. A rank-five-type term transports arbitrary
source proofs by replacing the handler with
$\lambda s\,u.h(g_d(s),u)$. Interpretation after transport and composition
of two such transports satisfy checked beta equations, without eta,
injectivity, surjectivity or semantic assumptions. This permits proof
movement between represented clause selections once their actual map is
constructed; it does not supply original-source coverage.

Dependent clause soundness needs more than a bare field table. For example,
$K(c)=\mathsf{Prem}_V(d,c)\to V(\mathsf{head}(d,c)):U_3$ can use
independent $\mathsf{Enum}_3(c)$ case evidence. `UniformGuardedHorn` provides
the corresponding generic interface: its handler takes an explicit
$G(d,c):U_g$ witness before the children. At each exact $g=3,4$, proof fibers
have rank $g+2$ and constructor/consumer types rank $g+3$, with checked
open-input laws and a fold beta equation retaining the guard. Both ranks
matter: $\mathsf{Enum}_3$ has rank four for nonempty alphabets but rank three
for the empty alphabet. There is no automatic lifting between them. Concrete
guard families, coverage and interpretation handlers still need construction;
the guard must not assert the desired soundness result.

The missing step is a **concrete instance**: represented clause and position
carriers with their projections, independent source-node coverage, and a
constructed interpretation handler valid for the represented resources.
The rank-one fragment carrier has not been identified with the rank-two raw
carrier or the higher-rank dictionary carrier. Abstracting these parameters
does not construct finite models uniformly or establish (BS). The finite
raw decoder and generic Horn interface are therefore complementary results,
not yet a composed source-to-soundness proof.

If normalization is used instead, scope preservation of `Syntax.subst`
must be strengthened to the annotated codomain/conversion coherence its
consumer needs. Native normalization and
`NativeCanonicity.normal_void_empty` do not supply that internal implication.
Avoid a same-index motive quantifying over its
own higher-rank graph proofs: `InvariantObstructions` rules out that rank
pattern. Raw intermediate terms in a valid conversion certificate need not
be typed or strongly normalizing; an untyped beta-loop is formalized.
Normalize the accepted subject, not those intermediates; use arbitrary-term
confluence or a negative transport law for the latter.

# Simplifications and logical scope

The direct route needs neither $H_{<\omega}$ nor an EA derivation.
It also does not inherently need a total budget selector, numerical capacity
comparison, global lookup functionality, full constructor inversion,
complete dependent typing reconstruction after every reduction, or a
normal-form output in the admitted domain. Prove the weaker local invariant
and coherence actually required by each consumer. Structural resources can
replace numeral conversion.

The PHOAS self-interpreter's `GenericFold` supplies typed composition once
an algebra is built; it does not prove that algebra's source-rule soundness.
Decoding a code of Bottom is useful only after independently obtaining that
code. No CPS theorem or rank-only self-interpreter is a prerequisite here.

Retain the supplied Zermelo revision's useful devices: complete-certificate
rank compression, independent generation guards, guarded finite elimination,
and continuation packaging of positive resources. Its proof-transport cost
claims must be checked for this representation. From
[Pakhomov's construction](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1907.00877v2), retain the
proof-local use of finite resources and the distinction between possible
internal consistency formulations. His transfer theorems are not axioms
of the present development.

The weak local finite satisfiability results in the
[combinatory note](../CombinatoryLogic-rk/CombinatoryPTS-rk.tex) are probably
highly relevant to investigating a non-Gödelian interpretation. They do not,
by themselves, prove the required quantitative internal soundness or a
failure of a derivability condition. Investigate
[Willard's stability framework](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1108.6330v8)
and its [deduction-system dependence](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1307.0150v2)
as a separate bridge: his self-justifying extensions do not give a proof
inside unchanged PTS-rk merely by analogy.

Run that audit now, not only after assembly. Identify the exact domain,
proof predicate, available proof-combination operations and applicable
diagonal/derivability conditions. Standard-instance coverage does not
identify two internal consistency sentences; a conventional or historical
claim needs a uniform internal translation. No escape from an applicable
incompleteness theorem is asserted.

Check original formation/typing, computation laws and fresh transitive
assumption reports; compare complete recursive kernel inventories with
pristine upstream. The resource-only counterexample does not refute the
entire programme.
